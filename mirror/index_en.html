ZXM-MoonSound

Specifications Project Files Firmware Programming Software Acknowledgments

ZXM-MoonSound is a sound card designed for playing complex musical compositions on computers with ZX BUS/Nemo Bus, utilizing frequency modulation (FM) and wavetable synthesis. The card is built on the Yamaha YMF278 (OPL4) chip, which can operate up to 18 channels of two-operator FM synthesis and play up to 24 channels of 12- or 16-bit digital audio.

This card is a replica of the Wozblaster sound card for MSX computers, presented by Argentine enthusiast Gustavo Iriarte (Ciro) at the end of 2012. In 2013, Evgeny Brychkov slightly modified it to fit into a standard Konami cartridge case. It should also be noted that Wozblaster was itself based on the MoonSound card developed by electronics engineer Henrik Gilvad, which was presented at the Tilburg computer fair in 1995.

In 2015, after demonstrating the musical capabilities of the Wozblaster card on the zx.pk.ru forum, which were quite impressive, the idea arose to try adapting it for our beloved ZX Spectrum. Preliminary analysis showed that a direct copy would not work due to some specific differences between the two platforms. Therefore, instead of the programmable logic chip (PAL) GAL16V8 used in the original, it was decided to use the more functional programmable logic chip (CPLD) EPM7032STC44, thereby eliminating structural differences. Additionally, for aesthetic reasons primarily—to make all chips surface-mount—it was decided to replace the 27C160 chip with an AM29F016. Programming the latter requires a cable that allows it to be programmed directly on the sound card board. The cable is wired according to the available programmer that can program the AM29F016. A small mixer for connecting external audio devices was also added to the card.

Revision 01 (2016)

In 2016, Vitaliy Mikhalkov (MV1971) organized the production of revision 01 boards. In this revision, the RCA connectors were changed, mounting holes for a bracket were added, and some through-hole electrolytic capacitors were replaced with their SMD equivalents. Additionally, an experiment was conducted to eliminate the initial click in the audio path—three new elements were added (two chips DD7, DD8 and one resistor R39). Unfortunately, the experiment was unsuccessful, so these three elements should not be installed. The programming connector for the flash ROM was also removed. Now the ROM is programmed directly on the board via the YMF278 chip. Boards of this revision have a red solder mask.

Specifications

OPL4Based on YMF278 chip
RAM1024 KB. Based on static memory modules (SRAM). Intended for loading user samples.
ROM2048 KB. Intended for storing fixed samples according to General MIDI standard.
Line OUT3.5mm Jack connector, 2 RCA (tulips), and 4-pin header for stereo audio output.
Circuit FeaturesUses CPLD EPM7032STC44 series to simplify circuit implementation.
Form FactorSlot-based, designed for ZX Bus/Nemo Bus. Note: boards have 62-pin slot connector, not 60-pin typical for ZX Bus/Nemo Bus.

Programming the Card

For first-time programming, ROM data must be loaded via the connector using an external programmer. For revision 01 boards, programming is done via the YMF278 chip using the MoonService utility.
